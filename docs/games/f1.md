# F1 25 – UDP telemetry

Implementation: `crates/sy-source-f1`. Source: EA's UDP specifications for
F1 25 (packet format **2025**) and the F1 25 **2026 Season Pack** (packet
format **2026**). EA's forum post refuses automated downloads, so the layouts
were read from a copy of EA's header in the `f1-packets` repository (read
only; not copied, not a dependency), see RESEARCH.md §1.2. Every parser is
checked against the packet sizes the spec states.

> **Status:** checked with six recordings (UDP format 2026, Windows, Nov
> 2026): time trials with the F1 2025 car (Catalunya), the F1 26 car
> (Suzuka), F2 2026 (Jeddah, with a pause), F2 2025 (Austria), F2 2024
> (Austria reversed), and a five-lap race with the F1 World car (Brazil,
> standing start, flashback). Lap times, sectors and validity match the
> game's own lap history in all of them. Still open: see *To verify*. Recordings go into
> `telemetry_examples/F125/` (raw `.syraw` files, *Settings → Keep raw
> recordings*); `crates/sy-capture/tests/f1_recordings.rs` replays them.

## Setting up the game

In F1 25: *Settings → Telemetry Settings*

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| UDP Telemetry | On |
| UDP Broadcast Mode | Off |
| UDP IP Address | `127.0.0.1` when the game runs on the same PC (also under Proton); otherwise the address of the PC running Stintory |
| UDP Port | 20777 (the app's default; changeable under *Settings → F1 25*) |
| UDP Send Rate | 60 Hz (lower rates work, with fewer samples per lap) |
| UDP Format | 2026 or 2025 |

**Consoles (PlayStation, Xbox) and other PCs:** the game sends the same
packets over the network. Enter the Stintory PC's address in the game, and
turn on *Accept data from other devices* in Stintory (it then listens on all
network interfaces; the firewall may ask). Nothing else is needed, so the
Linux app can record a console or a PC game too.

**Other telemetry apps:** only one program can listen on a port. Let
Stintory receive the packets and forward them (*Forward to other apps*,
e.g. `127.0.0.1:20778`), then point the other app at that port.

## What is recorded

- Only the player's car (docs/PLAN.md §5.2). Before a packet is spooled,
  every other car's entry in per-car arrays is zeroed, also the second
  player's in split screen. Packets about other cars (session history of
  others, tyre sets, final classification, lobby, lap positions, time trial
  rival) are dropped, as are events that name a car (overtakes, collisions,
  penalties, fastest lap, …). Forwarded packets are not changed.
- Nothing while spectating (that's another driver's car) or in the garage.
- One sample per car telemetry packet. Canonical channels: speed, throttle,
  brake, clutch, `steer_norm` (the steering input −1…1; the game doesn't
  report the wheel angle), gear, rpm, position, g-forces, yaw, fuel (kg),
  brake bias. Extras `f1.*`: DRS, ERS store/deploy mode/deployed this lap,
  engine temperature, front wheel angle, per wheel tyre surface and inner
  temperatures, brake temperatures, tyre pressures, slip ratio, suspension
  position; 2026 format also active aero mode and overtake mode.
- The car setup (exported only when chosen) and a player-only session info:
  UDP format, session type, formula, track id and length, assists, rule set,
  online or not, AI difficulty, team id, race number.

## Sessions, laps, validity

- A session is one `sessionUID` for the player's car index. In online
  sessions every driver has another index, so the ids of their sessions and
  laps differ. A new `sessionUID` or the event `SEND` ends the session.
  Keys: session `f1_25:<sessionUID hex>:<car index>`, track
  `f1_25:<trackId>`, car `f1_25:<teamId>/<formula>`.
- The session's wall-clock start is the receive time of its first packet
  minus its session time (stored with each packet in the spool, so a replay
  gives the same start).
- Lap times: when the lap number goes up, `lastLapTimeInMS` is the official
  time of the finished lap. Sectors 1 and 2 are the last values seen in that
  lap, sector 3 is the rest.
- A lap is invalid when `currentLapInvalid` was set **at the end** of the
  lap, or the player's session history clears its lap-valid bit. The state
  during the lap is not used, because a flashback can undo an invalidation.
- Flashback (event `FLBK`): the samples from the flashback's session time on
  are removed, and the lap is flagged *flashback*. In time trial the game
  invalidates the lap right after a flashback.
- Before the line the game counts lap 1 with a negative `lapDistance`.
  - Time trial (and every session that isn't a race): that's the out-lap
    from the pit exit, from almost a lap (Catalunya: 36 m after the line)
    to 185 m (Austria reversed). Stintory makes it lap 0, flagged out-lap,
    with the distance from the line. An invalidation in the out-lap carries
    over into lap 1 (the game's history agrees).
  - Race: that's the grid (Brazil: 42 m before the line). The game's time
    for lap 1 starts at the start, so lap 1 keeps these samples.
- The race's last lap: at the flag the game does **not** count up the lap
  number; only `lastLapTimeInMS` changes (and the result becomes
  "finished"). That ends the lap; the cool-down gets the next number.
- The game's "lap invalid" flag can already be set when a lap starts (seen
  after cutting the last chicane).
- Out-lap: the lap starts in the pit lane (`pitStatus` ≠ 0); in-lap: it ends
  there. Going back to the garage mid-lap ends the lap as incomplete.
- **Pause:** the game sends nothing while paused, and its session time
  stands still. The recording stays open while a session runs (from its
  first packet until `SEND`) for up to an hour of silence; after a session
  ended, 30 s of silence end the recording. Quitting a session sends
  "paused" and then `SEND`. Lap times include no pause.

## Axes and signs (checked)

- World X and Z are horizontal, Y is up. `pos_x` = X, `pos_y` = −Z gives an
  unmirrored map: steering left (`steer_norm` < 0) turns the path
  counter-clockwise. Mapping Z directly mirrored the map.
- The game's lateral g is negative in right-hand turns; `g_lat` is
  positive there (as for every game).
- The game's yaw is the direction of travel from +Z towards +X; `yaw` is
  the heading clockwise from +`pos_y` (π − yaw).

(its appendix was not readable). Team ids differ per formula and season:

| Formula | Team id | Car | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 F1 (2025 cars) | 0–9 | Mercedes, Ferrari, Red Bull Racing, Williams, Aston Martin, Alpine, Racing Bulls, Haas, McLaren, Sauber | 8 McLaren |
| 13 F1 26 | 476 | Mercedes | yes |
| 2 F2 | 160 / 466 / 499 | Rodin Motorsport (2024) / Campos Racing (2025) / Invicta Racing (2026) | yes |
| 8 F1 World | 41 | F1 World car | yes |

Other teams show by number ("team 470 (F1 26)") until we know them. Only
names depend on these tables; keys use the numbers.

## Reprocessing a recording

After a fix in the adapter, delete the session in the app and import the
raw `.syraw` file (*Import*, file type "Raw recording"): it is processed
again with the current code. Laps that are still stored are left as they
are. Several files chosen together are read as one recording in the order
they were recorded, so a session that an older version split in two (a
pause) comes back as one.
